# Tallybook business and financial controls — draft

Company-level policy snapshots govern refunds (including refund opt-out), approval of discounts and voids, drawer access, MoMo provider/account/reference requirements, staff roles and shifts, and cash-count tolerance. No global hardcoded refund or discount limit. Default new sale accountability: verified staff + open shift, with onboarding flow; historical records remain readable as legacy/unknown. All financial writes require the current company, role, shift, and suitable store/device provenance *inside their transactions*. Closing a shift captures cash expectation vs physically counted amount, keeping variances immutable; adjustments append corrections. Every void/reversal is a financial accounting event, NOT a confirmed external cash or MoMo refund. Repeated MoMo references require a scoped review; provider statement matching remains distinct from entered references. Failed PIN checks are rate-limited, never stored in plaintext. Cash drawers may only open from an authorized actor, reason and shift and must leave append-only audit evidence. Higher-level owner reports must only count attested/verified shop identities.
