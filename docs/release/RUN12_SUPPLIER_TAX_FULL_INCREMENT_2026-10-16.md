## Run 12 implementation

1. **Independent supplier ownership/settlement review:** `client/src/features/reports/run12SupplierFinanceAudit.mjs` verifies each saved purchase belongs to its actual original company supplier, purchase/event calendar timestamps are valid and settlement or recognition is not posted before original purchase, entry supplier/company/currency and amount match their source, and recognized payment/credit/outstanding conservation holds **per original currency**, never summing USD and GHS as though interchangeable. It invokes the existing independently reconstructed supplier financial report and tracks unrecognized purchases, recognized ones, manually attested payment counts, payments lacking external references and supplier credits missing independent credit-note references. An unreferenced credit is NOT presented as paid bank cash. Period recorded supplier payments remain by currency and separate from ordinary expenses; potential duplicate real-world disbursement across these two local streams is explicitly **unresolved**, not netted into a certified cash balance.
2. **Historical expense denomination preservation:** The supplier/expense audit identifies saved expenses with missing original currency or a denomination other than the active business. It does **not** rewrite those older records or pretend today's GHS was their original currency. Current single-currency management views already refuse to publish unsupported mixed/unknown-denomination financial aggregates; portable restore can retain legacy evidence instead of fabricating FX. Expense calendar and posting-time validity are checked.
3. **Supplier recognition and settlement posting strengthened:** `supplierPayables.ts` now reads the original supplier, company profile and purchase in the SAME Dexie RW transaction as `supplierPayableEntries` and staff authorization. Recognition and partial payment/credit each validate the proposed journal event against the existing immutable invoice ledger **before** saving, check original purchase posting chronology, revalidate company switching before and after staff audit, and retain existing exact replay/idempotency, cash-method, credit-vs-payment, staff/shift and Run 7 recovery barriers. Read-only supplier payables now validate original supplier ownership and active report permission and use indexed purchase lookups. The pure payable reducer now groups events in-place rather than repeatedly cloning each invoice's history, for a lower-spec Windows CPU/RAM baseline. Owner recognition of an unpaid old invoice remains an explicit MANUAL assertion; no bank/provider verification is created.
4. **Expense write hardening:** `createExpense` acquires the existing cross-tab shared Web Lock **before** opening its transaction. The actual expense RW scope now includes active company profile, staff and shifts. It validates staff/shift permissions, selected company and current company denomination inside the transaction, fails closed on new foreign-currency expense postings, and rechecks before commit. An EXACT previously saved expense submission-key retry can still return its historical original-currency record if the owner changed company currency later; fresh stale-currency entries are rejected. Original expense income/stock/customer/payable balances are not automatically changed.
5. **New purchase and sale FX barriers:** For new purchase receiving, saved business currency is checked within its existing stock/cost/receipt transaction; a previously accepted duplicate submission intent remains idempotently replayable even after a company currency-setting change. At sale checkout, a configured company likewise rejects a new sale in a different denomination before saving tender, tax, inventory or credit. Pre-business-profile historical test/legacy sales remain possible but do not inherit a fabricated company currency. No FX converter or custom tax duty is introduced.
6. **Original tax configuration ON or OFF preserved:** `client/src/features/reports/run12SaleTaxAudit.mjs` captures `Sale.taxConfigurationAtSale` inside the existing completed sale transaction. It records whether tax was expressly OFF or the exact originally registered single-rate policy in effect. The independent read-only validator reconstructs discount-before-tax, exact BigInt minor-unit inclusive/exclusive calculation and the invoice total, tax component, taxable amount and policy identity. A saved 'OFF' original with tax charged, or an original 'ON' without matching saved registered tax policy, fails closed. Changing current tax settings never retroactively alters a saved invoice. Sales from before Run 12 missing this snapshot are marked **historical tax configuration unverified**, NOT automatically exempt/no-tax; their earlier original tax arithmetic remains independently tested by `validateTaxSnapshot`. No input-tax credits, compound levies, exemptions, tax authority settlement or statutory filing is asserted.
7. **Reporting and portable backup preflight:** `getFinancialSummary` reads suppliers, purchases, purchase lines, payable entries, expenses and sales/tax evidence together in one company-scoped read transaction. Its `supplierFinanceAudit` and `taxEvidence` results and CSV/UI show exact period manual supplier payments by currency, unknown payables, unreferenced credit notes, original tax-on/off and legacy uncertainty. The longstanding collections-minus-expenses figure still **excludes supplier outflows** rather than double counting possible duplicated external disbursements. `backup.ts` adds both supplier and tax evidence audits to the whole-ledger **prewrite** validation; malformed new tax proof or wrong supplier/chronology/currency references reject before data replacement. Existing Run 9 FIFO cost, Run 10 customer debt/wallet and Run 11 refund authority checks remain active.
8. **Tax settings and legal labels:** Business tax UI explains that original ON/OFF receipts are snapshotted and warns that registered single-rate policy does not establish jurisdiction-specific legal rate, exempt items, input tax, multiple levies or actual tax filing. No company is forced to adopt Ghana/GHS; company currency remains a configurable setting.

## Material open acceptance limitations

- Manual supplier payment or supplier credit entry does not prove a bank/MoMo/provider remittance, refund or external credit note. References are local notes, not signature-verified settlement. Combining a supplier payment with an ordinary expense could double count one real disbursement; the app deliberately refuses to assert a unified cash-flow net without independent reconciliation.
- Unknown liabilities remain unknown until a business manually attests recognition. Absence of historic invoice event or tax settings never implies zero debt, zero tax, legal exemption or supplier payment.
- Company currency changes require explicit historical accounting/revaluation approval before mixed-denomination reporting; no exchange rate or FX is invented. Expense, sale and purchase new writes enforce current currency when an active profile is established.
- Tax remains single-rate registered owner policy only; no statutory filing, input credits, returns, exemptions or multi-levy support. Mandatory tax and consumer laws require jurisdiction-specific review.
- Offline-first browser IndexedDB and legacy no-staff owner bootstrap are not cryptographically secure custody. All relevant authorization/device crashes, recovery Web Locks, quota and other-browser restore remain unaccepted. No staging or cloud finance release is authorized.
