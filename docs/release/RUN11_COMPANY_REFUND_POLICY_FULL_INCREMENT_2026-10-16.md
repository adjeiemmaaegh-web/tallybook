## Actual source changes

1. **Per-company refund rules, default OFF.** New `client/src/features/business/returnPolicy.mjs` defines strict company-specific settings: disabled entirely, optionally allowed audited full-sale reversals, optionally allowed physically confirmed restockable cash-only partial returns, and a configurable **1–3650 day** age limit. This is an application policy, not a claim to override mandatory local consumer law. Companies have independent rules. No universal Ghana/GHS/refund preset is imposed.
2. **Append-only policy history in the existing company profile.** Owner-controlled `returnPolicyHistory` stores chronological, strictly increasing numbered revisions, effective UTC instants, allowed lanes, maximum return age, owner reason and optional authorized staff actor ID. The dedicated `updateCompanyReturnPolicy` obtains shared origin write coordination **outside** Dexie, revalidates company owner/staff permission, the pending Run 7 recovery barrier and company switching, then appends the new revision and staffed policy-change audit in **one IndexedDB transaction**. Ordinary `saveBusinessProfile` preserves policy history; form edits cannot silently wipe it. The historical company profile row is backed up without a new schema table or version bump.
3. **Immutable original-sale terms.** `completeSale` snapshots the company refund terms in `Sale.returnPolicyAtSale` within its existing stock/money write transaction. New/legacy pre-setup sales with no company policy receive an explicit **disabled revision 0** snapshot rather than implied permission. Pre-Run 11 sales missing this snapshot are retained as legacy and **cannot** be automatically promoted to eligible refunds.
4. **Dual-time authorization inside both actual refund paths.** `executeLatestSaleReversal` and `recordConfirmedCashPartialReturn` read the actual active business profile **inside their existing Dexie write transactions**, alongside original sale, staff/shift, sale money, batch and FIFO evidence. `authorizeReturnPolicy` requires **both** eligible original sale-time terms and the currently enabled matching company lane, enforces the stricter of the saved sale/current age limits, checks nonfuture company revisions and sale/posting chronology, and records `policyEvidence` (sale-policy snapshot, decision-policy snapshot, return kind and exact posting time) directly in the immutable signed reversal / cash return. Existing staff permission, original receipt/payment, return quantity, tax, FIFO, batch, customer/credit/wallet/points and cash-tender restrictions are not bypassed. Full reversal additionally rechecks company and permission before final posting.
5. **Read-only preview and retail UI controls.** Both full reversal assistant preview and cash-partial review now refuse an ineligible company/sale rather than implying payment could be completed. Recent sales show the read-only cash-return button only when the company and sale have supported partial-return authority. The dedicated `CompanyReturnPolicySettings` section in `BusinessSetup` allows business owners to choose **No refunds** or permitted full/partial paths, configure the return window, enter an audit reason, review revision counts and explicitly confirm the decision. The UI warns that statutory consumer rights may override application terms. No remote provider refund UI is activated.
6. **Independent historical policy audit.** `returnPolicyAudit.mjs` validates exact revision history and cross-checks any saved sale-time terms and refund decision snapshots against historical company versions, correct original sale, posting order and the revision effective **at the moment** of the refund. Later disabling the policy does **not** erase or retroactively invalidate an authorized earlier posted refund. Missing pre-Run 11 policy evidence remains **legacy/unverified**, not automatically assigned today's rule or discarded.
7. **Backup and reporting.** `parseBackup` invokes policy audit as part of the existing read-only whole-ledger preflight **before any company data replacement**; modified policy history, mismatched sale/return snapshot, foreign original sale or future-dated policy is refused. Management reports and CSV disclose verified-policy refunds, historical refund entries with unknown original terms, old sales without return-policy snapshots, and active policy revision rather than declaring their authorization proven. Existing Run 9 FIFO cash/tax and Run 10 credit/prepaid reconciliations remain in place.

**Limits intentionally retained:** This is NOT a generic payment-provider refund engine; an enabled policy does not make credit/partial wallet/MoMo/card refunds, damaged goods, service returns or unsupported batch restocks safe. The cash-partial posting lane remains **local, physically confirmed, cash-only, noncustomer walk-in, restockable FIFO**. A full reversal is an existing signed local ledger correction; no remote payout is executed. Device-local owner PIN/bootstrap and browser IndexedDB are not tamper-proof. Legal consumer rights and local tax/refund laws require review.
