# Current status (updated 2026-11-11)

**Production-readiness verdict (2026-11-11): ready for a controlled pilot, not yet an open launch.** The evidence, the gaps and who closes each, the go-live steps and the rollback are in [PRODUCTION_READINESS_2026-11-11.md](PRODUCTION_READINESS_2026-11-11.md).

This is the single page to read first. Everything else under `docs/release/` is a dated snapshot of an earlier stage and is kept as evidence only. In particular `blockers.json` and `traceability.json` are the October V19 registers: automated QA scripts read them, so they are not edited by hand, and their "NO-GO / INCOMPLETE" wording does not describe today's shared shop.

## Open development work

Quick wins: done (zoom, PWA install, in-app confirmations, supplier archive, documentation pass, one-step transfer, WhatsApp send for receipts, balances and payment confirmations). The owner/employee choice at sign-up already existed; business type needs a server field and moves into the product-fields migration.

Sales search with day, store, payment and text filters plus paging is live in production: migrations `20261110120000` (indexes) and `20261110130000` (search) were applied on 2026-11-10 (recorded as `shop_sales_hot_path_indexes` and `shop_sales_search`) and checked with a rolled-back dry run against the real data. The app falls back to the latest 50 sales, labelled, if it ever talks to a server without them. Rollback scripts are in `supabase/rollback`.

Done in code: receipt share, WhatsApp and print in the shared shop, and free problem reporting. A "Report a problem" button (shared shop footer and the error screen) shows scrubbed error details plus the person's own note and lets them send it by WhatsApp, the share sheet or copy. Nothing is uploaded by Tallybook and there is no paid service. Optional settings: `VITE_TALLYBOOK_SUPPORT_WHATSAPP` (the number it opens) and `VITE_TALLYBOOK_ERROR_REPORT_URL` (automatic delivery to an https endpoint; unset by default). Server-side errors are visible for free in the Supabase logs.

Live: shared-shop product details (barcode, category, goods or service, stock counting, low-stock level, owner-only cost) with the owner's product form and a till that sells services without stock. Migration `20261110140000` (re-declares the live sale and refund functions) was applied to production on 2026-11-11 and its function definitions were checked identical to the repository. The app still works against an older server (every product stays a counted good). Rollback: `supabase/rollback/20261110140000_shop_product_details_down.sql`.

Barcode scan at the shared till: a search box that also takes camera scans (where the browser supports them) and USB or Bluetooth scanners (they type the code and press Enter). A barcode match wins; typed text adds the one product it matches or asks the person to choose; long digit-only codes are never matched by name. It needs the barcode field from the product-details step, so it is useful once that migration is applied.

CSV product import for the shared shop (Stock tab, "Import products from a file"): plain prices, columns in any order, a downloadable template, a preview that lists every skipped row with its reason, one confirmation, progress, and a safe re-run (existing names and barcodes are skipped, never changed). Up to 500 products and 2 MB per file. It uses the product-details action, so it needs that migration applied.

Park and resume a cart at the till: "Set this cart aside" keeps up to five carts per store on this device only (sealed, per person and business, cleared on sign-out, dropped after a day). Resuming re-checks today's products and stock, so nothing stale is sold. Nothing is recorded or reserved while a cart is parked, and a cart is only cleared from the screen once the device has confirmed it stored it.

Stock console (Stock tab): stock value at cost (products with no cost are left out and counted), products, units on hand, low and out-of-stock counts for the selected store, plus filter chips and a search box.

Daily and end-of-day reports (new Reports tab for owners, "My day" for attendants): sales, refunds, net sales, money taken by method, repayments, supplier payments (owners), best sellers, sales per cashier (owners) and the cash that should be in the till, for today, yesterday, the last 7 days or this month, per store or all. It can be shared as text or sent on WhatsApp. The server read is migration `20261110150000` (`dailyReport`, read-only, rollback in `supabase/rollback`), applied to production on 2026-11-11.

Attendants who count stock and receive goods (tier 4, item 11): the owner can switch on "Can count stock and receive goods" for an individual attendant under Team (off by default). That attendant gets Stock counts and Receive goods tabs for the stores they are assigned to only; they see what each open order still expects but never a cost, and cannot create orders, pay suppliers or move stock between stores. Every movement keeps their name. Migration `20261111100000` re-declares the owner-only stock and purchase functions with the gate widened for those two features and nothing else, and adds a wrapper that checks the switch and the store assignment first. Rollback in `supabase/rollback`. Applied to production on 2026-11-11; an older server simply hides the tabs.

Customers (tier 4, item 12): the owner can edit a customer's name and phone, archive a customer who owes nothing (hidden from the list and the till, history kept, restorable) and every statement can be shared, sent on WhatsApp to the customer's number or printed. Migration `20261111110000` adds the archive table, the archive actions and a database rule that refuses a new credit sale to an archived customer; rollback in `supabase/rollback`. Applied to production on 2026-11-11; an older server shows every customer, as before.

Employee short-code join (tier 4, N5): the owner makes an 8-character join code under Team (shown once, kept only as a hash, works once, expires after 24 hours, at most 5 open, cancellable). A signed-in employee types it on the "Join your employer's shop" screen and becomes an attendant with no permissions and no store. Five wrong guesses lock that account for 15 minutes. The email invitation (long code) path is unchanged. Migration `20261111120000`, rollback in `supabase/rollback`. Applied to production on 2026-11-11.

Expenses (tier 4, item 17): an owner-only Expenses tab records money spent running the shop (category, amount, how paid, date, optional store and note). Records are append-only; a mistake is cancelled with a reason and stays visible. The daily report gains an Expenses block (cancelled ones left out) and cash expenses count as cash that left the till. Expenses never touch stock, sales, customers or suppliers, and nothing computes profit yet (that is item 16). Attendants cannot record expenses. Migration `20261111130000` (also re-declares the report function with the new block), rollback in `supabase/rollback`. Applied to production on 2026-11-11; an older server shows reports without expenses.

Refund requests (tier 4, item 18): a cashier can ask the owner for a refund on a sale they made (whole sale or some items, with a reason and whether the goods can go back in stock). The owner sees waiting requests under Refund requests (with a count on the tab) and approves or declines; approving runs the existing refund, with every policy check, in the same transaction, so a request only reads "approved" if the refund was posted. One open request per sale, five per cashier; requests are kept as history. Migration `20261111140000`, rollback in `supabase/rollback`. Applied to production on 2026-11-11; an older server simply has no requests.

Profit and margin (tier 5, item 16): each sale line now remembers what the item cost when it was sold, so changing a cost later never rewrites past profit. Reports gains an owner-only Profit section: gross profit and margin on the sales whose cost is known, cost of goods (restocked refunds give their cost back, unrestocked goods stay a cost), expenses, net profit, best earners, a plain warning for sales with no cost recorded (all sales before this change), and a spreadsheet download and share. Supplier payments are not an expense. Migration `20261111150000` (adds a column and a trigger on sale lines, plus the read), rollback in `supabase/rollback` (the recorded costs are lost if it is rolled back). Applied to production on 2026-11-11 (function definitions checked identical to the repository).

Ask (tier 5, N8): a new Ask tab answers plain questions from the shop's own numbers: what is low or out of stock (per store, using each product's own level), how many of something, a price, sales today / yesterday / this week / this month, best sellers, who owes money, and (owner only) profit. It understands everyday English and a little pidgin, and says so when it doesn't. It is read-only. An owner can also ask it to draft an order for what is low: it tops each item up to twice its low-stock level for the store that needs most, and opens the Purchasing form pre-filled (costs the owner already recorded are filled in); nothing is ordered until the owner chooses a supplier and posts it. Attendants get the same tab without owner questions (no profit, no drafts, balances only if allowed to handle credit). Stock, prices and balances work offline; sales and profit need a connection. No server change.

Quotations (tier 5, N10): a Quotes tab for owners and attendants. A quotation is an offer for a customer (a saved customer if the person may use them, otherwise a name): items and quantities, how many days it is good for (up to 90) and a note. The server fills in today's prices and keeps them on the quotation, numbers quotations per business, and shows an owner everyone's quotations and an attendant their own. It can be shared, sent on WhatsApp (to the customer's number if saved) or printed; marked accepted, declined or cancelled; and "Start a sale from this" loads its items at the till. It is marked Sold only if the sale recorded is exactly those items. A quotation holds no stock and moves no money, expired ones can no longer be accepted or sold, and once closed it stays closed. Migration `20261111160000`, rollback in `supabase/rollback`. **Not applied to production yet**; an older server simply has no Quotes tab data (the tab shows an error).

Accounts (tier 5, N17): an owner-only Accounts tab follows the money through four accounts (cash, mobile money, card, bank). For today, yesterday, the last 7 days or this month it shows what came in and went out of each one, built only from records the shop already keeps (sales by how they were paid, customers' payments, refunds paid back, supplier payments, expenses, and transfers between accounts such as cash banked). Credit that customers have not paid is not money in any account; it is shown, with what the shop owes suppliers and stock at cost, under "What is owed, and stock". Reconciliation: the owner counts what is really there and saves it. The first count sets the account's starting balance; each later count is compared with the previous count plus everything recorded since, and the difference (over or short) is kept. Counts and transfers are append-only; a transfer is cancelled with a reason. A spreadsheet of the period can be downloaded. Together with Profit this is the shop's profit and loss, cash position and reconciliation; it is not a full double-entry ledger, tax return or bank feed. Migration `20261111170000`, rollback in `supabase/rollback`. **Not applied to production yet.** Also fixed on the way: a loss in the profit spreadsheet came out as text instead of a negative number, and quotation lines now keep the order they were written in.

In-app guide (tier 5, N18): a searchable Help and questions panel at the foot of the shop page, with answers for owners and attendants that describe only what the app does today. It is part of the app, so it works offline.

Migration hygiene: every migration from `20261106120000` onward now has a rollback script (`supabase/rollback`, run newest first), proven by a test that rolls the whole chain back and applies it again. `supabase/MIGRATION_HISTORY.md` maps production's recorded versions to the repository files and lists what the test project is missing. The test project has **not** been brought up to production (it needs about a dozen migrations applied by hand).

Faster first load: the local ledger's code (providers, shell, routes, offline assistant, backup, sales, forms) now loads only on its own routes, not on `/shop`. First load of the shared shop went from about 440 KB to about 231 KB gzipped; the device routes cost the same as before. The account-change staff lock in `App.tsx` is untouched. A tooltip provider and the currency list moved out of the shared path too.

Phone-width pass on the shared shop: a browser check (`qa/shop-phone-layout.spec.mjs`, run by CI) loads the real app at 360 and 320 px as an owner and as an attendant, with only the Supabase network mocked, visits every tab, and fails if anything makes the page scroll sideways or stick out, or if a field is under 16 px (which makes iPhones zoom in). It found and fixed three real problems: pages up to 64 px too wide on Sell and Stock, every field at 13.6 px, and Sales amounts clipped. Not covered: iPhone Safari itself, real keyboards and slow networks.

Shared shop catching up with the device app: product fields (barcode, category, goods or service, stock tracking, cost), barcode scan at the till, receipt print and share, CSV product import, sales filters and search, park and resume cart, daily and end-of-day reports, inventory console, attendant stock counts and receiving, customer edit and archive, expenses, employee short-code join and attendant refund requests are done (tiers 1 to 4). Profit and margin and the in-app guide are done (tier 5). Still to do (tier 5): loyalty, Bluetooth printing, product images and variants; app-store packaging last.

Later: assistant over shared-shop data, quotations and loyalty in the shared shop, Bluetooth receipt printing, product images and variants, accounting export, app-store packaging.

## Final QA audit (2026-11-11)

Run on the tip of `feature/tier4-attendant-stock`:

- **Passing:** type check; production build; 1,458 client tests; 651 native tests in `qa/` plus 419 server tests; browser suites for release acceptance, sign-in, offline checkout and the shared shop at phone width (owner and attendant, every tab); `pnpm audit` (one low advisory, below the CI threshold).
- **Accessibility:** an automated WCAG 2.0 A and AA scan of every shared-shop tab at phone width found one issue, low-contrast grey text (4.1:1 to 4.4:1 against the 4.5:1 required) and out-of-stock rows faded to 2:1. Fixed by darkening the muted-text and success colours and styling unavailable items by colour instead of fading them. The scan now finds nothing. It was run once with a throwaway tool and is not part of CI.
- **Fixed in the audit:** seven source-reading contract tests in `qa/` still looked for the device app's routes in `App.tsx` after they moved into `DeviceWorkspace.tsx` (the CI native step would have failed); the shop refresh asked the server for five things one after another after every action and now asks for the four optional ones together; a test of attendants never seeing costs that failed about one run in a thousand because a random id can contain "800" (it now inspects fields, not text); a timing gap in the accounts migration where a sale waiting for the ledger lock could be stamped before a count's cut-off yet commit after it, and so be missed by two counts in a row (a count now covers records up to 3 seconds before it was saved; not yet applied to production, so nothing live was affected).
- **Known and accepted:** the database advisor reports RLS-without-policy on the shop tables (intended: they are reachable only through the `tallybook_shop*` functions), the signed-in-callable `SECURITY DEFINER` entry points (intended: each checks the caller itself), unindexed foreign keys on audit columns and a few unused indexes (informational, none matter at today's volume), and leaked-password protection being off in Auth (a setting for the security pass). Quotation validity uses the server's date, so it can differ from a shop's local date by a few hours outside Ghana time.
- **Not covered:** a security review (parked by agreement), real devices and slow networks, iPhone Safari, and anything needing a printer.
