# Demo Business Scenario

Use this scenario only in a separate browser profile, a cleared test device, or after taking a fresh backup. It is a guide for owner testing and is **not** seeded into your real records.

| Test record | Suggested value |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Release Test Kiosk |
| Currency | Your normal working currency |
| Product A | Soap — selling price 10.00 — stock 5 |
| Product B | Water — selling price 5.00 — stock 4 |
| Customer | Test Customer — use a clearly recognisable test phone number only |

Record one cash sale of Soap, then one part-paid Water sale to Test Customer. Open the customer to record the remaining repayment. Confirm the dashboard and Reports show the saved values. Download a backup, change a product quantity, then restore the backup and confirm the original test records return.

> Do not use this scenario in a live business record unless you remove the test entries through the normal owner workflow before relying on reports.
