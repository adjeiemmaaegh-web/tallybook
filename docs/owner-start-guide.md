# Owner Start Guide

## Your first working record

Start by opening **Business** and saving the business name, country, currency, and business type. Next, open **Stock** and add each product with the selling price, starting quantity, and low-stock point. Use **Customers** only for people whose details you want to keep, especially customers who may pay later.

| Daily task | Where to go | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record a cash sale | Sales | Add product, confirm amount received, then complete the sale once. |
| Record part payment or credit | Sales | Choose a named customer, select the payment plan, and check the remaining amount. |
| Record later payment | Customers | Open the customer, record repayment, and confirm the balance changes. |
| Review the day | Today or Reports | Read completed sales, money received, current debt, and low-stock attention. |
| Keep a copy | Backup | Download a fresh local backup after important work. |

## Safe daily habit

Before closing the app after a busy day, open **Backup**, download a copy, and keep it in a private location you control. If the header says **Working offline**, you can continue using local records. When connectivity returns, the app only confirms the connection; it does not upload anything.
